# 画像素材の制作記録

内蔵`image_gen`を使用。参照した世界観はユーザー提供のSTREAM CLIMBERデザイン案です。選択した素材をすべてプロジェクトへコピーしています。

## `public/assets/sky-tower.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a polished wide 1536x1024 anime game title background for STREAM CLIMBER. Celestial blue sky, glowing cumulus clouds, an enormous fantastical streaming broadcast tower on the RIGHT reaching upward crowned with a luminous gold crown, cyan and violet holographic cat-face screens, floating gift boxes and microphones, sleek sci-fi details. LEFT half mostly open deep blue sky to support HTML title typography. Lower right foreground a small white-haired cat-ear headset anime streamer girl riding a glossy white spherical futuristic capsule with cyan light trims and original blue cat emblem, holding a wired black microphone on a long cable reaching upward. Premium luminous anime key visual, detailed painterly environment, bright blue/cyan/lilac highlights, dramatic upward perspective, optimistic and dreamy. No text, no typography, no UI, no panels, no watermark. Reference overall visual concept: user supplied STREAM CLIMBER concept board, recreate as one full-bleed cohesive artwork.

## `public/assets/player.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent 2D game character sprite. One single isolated full character, centered, entire silhouette with generous empty transparent padding. A cute young adult anime female streamer with long flowing silver-white hair tinted lavender, blue eyes, futuristic black cat-ear headset with cyan illumination, black white and turquoise streamer jacket, seated waist-deep inside a small round glossy white futuristic floating broadcast capsule. Capsule round bottom cyan glowing rim and original blue square smiling cat emblem on front, black underside. Three-quarter front view looking right. One right arm reaches out to right to hold a cable (do not draw cable or microphone, they are separate physics objects). Compact chibi proportions, head to capsule width ratio 0.55, detailed beautiful clean anime cel shaded game sprite, cyan purple rim lights. Character and capsule together as one sprite. Truly transparent alpha background. No floor, no shadows outside silhouette, no text, no panels, no border, no second character.

注：生成結果の背景に市松模様が残ったため、実装ではシルエットクリップを使用しています。透明化の再生成も試しましたが採用しませんでした。

Oversized lavender mugs, white headphones and a ring light near the far left and right edges, luminous cyan and violet PC monitors, pastel gift boxes on shelves and hanging cables, soft puffy clouds drifting between the giant studio furniture, dreamy distant screens glowing with original smiling cat icons. Beautiful detailed anime background painting, brilliant daylight, volumetric soft clouds, cyan lavender blue palette, cozy futuristic streamer room at impossible scale, strong parallax depth. Center 60 percent relatively clear soft sky with distant architecture to keep small foreground gameplay legible. Objects mostly peripheral and in background. No characters, no people, no humanoids, no text, no typography, no UI, no HUD, no frame. Match a premium Japanese anime streaming fantasy key visual.
